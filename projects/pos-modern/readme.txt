POS Modern is a complete point-of-sale and business management system designed to centralize sales, purchasing, stock, invoicing, payments, customers, suppliers, and financial operations in one application.
It includes a wide range of business tools, including:
- Point of Sale – Process sales using barcode scanning, product search, discounts, suspended transactions, customer selection, and multiple payment methods.
- Sales Transactions – Manage sales history, transaction details, returns, payments, and receipts.
- Purchasing – Create and manage purchases, purchase orders, supplier transactions, and incoming stock.
- Invoicing – Generate and manage invoices linked to customers, sales, and business transactions.
- Product & Inventory Management – Manage products, categories, units, barcodes, prices, stock quantities, batches, serial numbers, and expiration tracking.
- Warehouse Management – Track and manage inventory across one or multiple warehouses.
- Customer Management – Maintain customer records, balances, receivables, and transaction history.
- Supplier Management – Manage suppliers, payable balances, purchases, and account activity.
- Cash Register Management – Open and close registers, record cash inflows and outflows, and reconcile cash sessions.
- Payments Management – Track payments received, outgoing payments, outstanding balances, and payment methods.
- Reports & Analytics – Monitor sales, purchases, revenue, stock, customers, suppliers, cash flow, and financial performance.
- Import & Export Tools – Import and export business data for administration, migration, and reporting.
- Business Settings – Configure currencies, warehouses, users, printing, stock rules, registers, and application preferences.
POS Modern provides a centralized solution for managing the full commercial workflow, from purchasing and inventory to sales, invoicing, payments, and financial reporting
