Parking Roissy is a modern parking reservation and management platform designed for an airport parking and valet service.
It combines a public booking website with a complete administration dashboard for managing reservations and parking operations.
Key features include:
- Online Reservations – Customers can book parking by entering personal, vehicle, travel, and arrival/departure information.
- Parking Management – Track available, occupied, and pending parking spaces in real time.
- Reservation Management – Manage current, confirmed, completed, and cancelled reservations.
- Pricing Management – Configure parking rates based on duration and additional service options.
- Promo Codes & Coupons – Create percentage or fixed-value discounts with expiration dates and usage limits.
- Parking Statistics – Monitor vehicle entries, exits, occupancy, and daily activity.
- Admin Dashboard – Centralized interface for managing clients, reservations, parking spaces, pricing, and promotions.
- Customer Website – Public landing page with pricing, advantages, contact information, location, and direct access to the reservation form.
Parking Roissy provides a complete digital solution for handling both customer bookings and the daily operational management of an airport parking service.
