DocScript is a modern medical practice management system designed to simplify and centralize the daily operations of healthcare professionals and medical clinics.
It provides a complete set of tools for managing the entire workflow of a medical practice, including:
- Appointment Management – Schedule, organize, and track patient appointments efficiently.
- Patient Management – Maintain structured patient records, medical information, and consultation history.
- Prescription & e-Prescription Management – Create, manage, and track modern digital prescriptions.
- Medical Calculators – Integrated clinical calculators to assist healthcare professionals during consultations.
- Financial Management – Track payments, expenses, revenue, and other financial activities.
- Role & Permission Management – Control access for doctors, assistants, administrators, and other staff members.
- Inventory Management – Monitor medical supplies, products, stock levels, and inventory movements.
DocScript aims to provide healthcare professionals with a centralized, secure, and modern platform for managing their practice, patients, clinical workflows, and administrative operations.
