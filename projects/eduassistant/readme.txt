EduAssistant is a modern educational productivity platform designed to simplify and improve the way students create and organize academic solutions, especially for people with dysgraphia or handwriting difficulties.
It provides a complete set of tools for creating clear, structured, and professional digital solutions, including:
- Text Editor – Write and format explanations, answers, notes, and academic content efficiently.
- Mathematical Writing Tools – Insert formulas, symbols, operators, Greek letters, and other mathematical notation.
- Table Generator – Create structured tables, value tables, and organized data layouts quickly.
- Graph Assistant – Draw graphs, axes, lines, points, shapes, labels, and other graphical elements.
- Document Preview – View the original course, exercise, or reference document while writing the solution.
- Subject & Chapter Management – Organize content by subject, chapter, folder, and exercise.
- Solution Management – Save, edit, organize, and reuse completed academic solutions.
EduAssistant was originally designed to help people with dysgraphia overcome the difficulties of handwritten work, but it can also be used by any student, teacher, or learner who wants a faster, cleaner, and more organized way to create digital solutions for mathematics, science, programming, and other academic subjects.
