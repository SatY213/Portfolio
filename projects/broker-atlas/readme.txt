Broker Atlas is a modern data discovery and intelligence workspace for collecting, processing, verifying, and organizing professional contact data.
It provides a complete workflow for managing large-scale data collection, including:
- Discovery – Find and collect relevant public or licensed sources.
- People Directory – Organize discovered individuals and track qualification status.
- Source Management – Keep a clear record of where each piece of information comes from.
- Automated Processing Pipeline – Run background jobs for scraping, enrichment, verification, and data processing.
- Email & Phone Enrichment – Add and verify contact information when available.
- Analytics – Monitor coverage, data quality, qualification rates, and collection progress.
- Job Monitoring – Track queue activity, failures, retries, and processing status.
- Exports – Generate filtered CSV or JSON datasets for further use.
Broker Atlas focuses on data quality, traceability, and automation, providing a structured workspace for turning raw public data into verified and usable records.
