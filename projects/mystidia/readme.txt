Mystidia is a modern educational platform designed for medical students to access, organize, and study academic content in one place.
It provides a complete set of tools for managing medical learning resources, including:
- Structured Medical Program – Organize content by academic year, unit, module, chapter, and course.
- Course & E-book Access – Browse medical courses, books, PDFs, and learning materials.
- Integrated Glossary – Access medical terms, definitions, images, and related explanations.
- PDF Study Tools – Read documents, highlight text, add notes, and interact with course content.
- AI Assistance – Ask questions directly while studying and get contextual help from course material.
- Personal Study Space – Save and organize files and resources in a personal “backpack”.
- Subscription System – Manage annual or resident access plans and activation codes.
- Admin Dashboard – Manage users, subscriptions, academic content, glossaries, e-books, comments, and platform resources.
Mystidia aims to provide medical students with a centralized and interactive learning environment that combines structured content, study tools, AI assistance, and resource management in a single platform.
